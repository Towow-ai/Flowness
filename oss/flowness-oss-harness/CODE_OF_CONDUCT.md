# Community conduct

Flowness should be a place where people can challenge technical claims without
attacking one another. Participants are expected to be respectful, specific, and
evidence-oriented.

Acceptable participation includes good-faith disagreement, reproducible negative
results, questions from newcomers, and criticism of architecture or claims.
Unacceptable participation includes harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of private data, and sustained
personal attacks.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Enforcement decisions should record the behavior at issue, the action taken, and
an appeal path where practical. Security reports and private-data incidents must
use the private reporting path described in `SECURITY.md`, not a public issue.

This project-authored policy is intentionally short and does not claim affiliation
with or adoption of a third-party code-of-conduct template.
